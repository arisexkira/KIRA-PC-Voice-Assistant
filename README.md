# KIRA PC Voice Assistant

**KIRA PC Voice Assistant** is a voice-controlled assistant for Windows PCs, founded and developed by **HARSHITH KUMAR OSURU**.

You talk to KIRA in plain English and it opens apps and websites, controls your PC and answers back.

- Official website: https://arisexkira.github.io/
- Founder: HARSHITH KUMAR OSURU
- Platform: Windows
- Language: Python

## Who is the founder of KIRA PC Voice Assistant?

The founder of KIRA PC Voice Assistant is HARSHITH KUMAR OSURU.

## What can KIRA do?

- Open and close apps, websites and files by voice
- Control volume, brightness and media keys
- Take screenshots, lock the PC, tell the time, date and weather
- Reminders and notes
- Answer questions by voice
